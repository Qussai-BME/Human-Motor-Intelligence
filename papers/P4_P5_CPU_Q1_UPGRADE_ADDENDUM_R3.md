# P4/P5 CPU-First Q1 Upgrade Addendum
## V2 Recovery R3 Extension 001–002

## Purpose

This addendum records prospective CPU-only extensions to the frozen P4 and P5 manuscripts. It is not a silent revision of the frozen papers and does not replace their primary results.

## P4

A log-Euclidean covariance representation with shrinkage LDA was executed under the 54-subject subject-wise LOSO protocol. Mean subject-level macro-F1 was 0.35408 (SD 0.04930), with mean accuracy and balanced accuracy of 0.50065 (SD 0.00721). The independent rerun was byte-identical. The result demonstrates executable representation-geometry evidence under a new namespace; it does not establish superiority over the frozen P4 classifier set and should not be pooled with it.

The fold-local alignment ablation remains registered but no result was released because the current CPU implementation exceeded the available execution window. Calibration, perturbation robustness, and cross-dataset evaluation remain separate gates.

## P5

Entropy-confidence-weighted fusion was executed on the 24-subject amended Jeong cache. Mean subject-level macro-F1 was 0.08021 (SD 0.03033), compared with EEG-only 0.08140, static mean fusion 0.08051, and EMG-only 0.04844. The weighted-fusion primary/rerun output was byte-identical. The result does not establish a fusion benefit over EEG-only.

A second extension applied deterministic feature-group dropout at 0%, 10%, and 20% in EEG and EMG arms and explicit missing-modality fallback states. The weighted-fusion macro-F1 was 0.08040/0.07658/0.07540 under EEG dropout and 0.08040/0.08037/0.08037 under EMG dropout. Missing-EMG fallback produced 0.08127 and missing-EEG fallback 0.04844. These are feature-cache perturbation outcomes, not raw hardware robustness or physiological mechanism evidence.

## Editorial disposition

The frozen P4/P5 manuscripts may cite this addendum only as new extension evidence. No conclusion should say that Riemannian alignment improved generalization, that confidence weighting improved fusion, or that missing-modality fallback guarantees robustness. P3 and P6 R3 remain independent gates.

## R3-005 Calibration and Reliability Analysis

The P5 clean LOSO calibration extension evaluated EEG-only, EMG-only, static-mean fusion, confidence-weighted fusion, and confidence-selective fusion. The output was independently regenerated byte-for-byte under the same cache, seed, and protocol. Static-mean fusion showed the lowest Brier score among fusion conditions (0.919149) and essentially the same ECE as EMG-only (0.044729 versus 0.044483). Confidence-weighted fusion yielded Brier 0.938161 and ECE 0.085202, whereas confidence-selective fusion yielded Brier 0.948498 and ECE 0.106809. Their accuracies were both 0.110909, below EEG-only at 0.114318 and static-mean fusion at 0.112727. Confidence–correctness associations were near zero across conditions. Accordingly, this cohort does not validate the present confidence heuristic as a reliability-aware fusion improvement. The finding is cohort- and protocol-specific and should motivate fold-local learned calibration in future work rather than a deployment claim.

## R3-006 P4 Robustness Status

A clean-train/corrupted-test robustness implementation was initiated for the selected temporal representation with deterministic channel-group dropout, additive noise, and temporal jitter. It exceeded the available CPU execution budget before writing any result artifact and was stopped. This is an execution non-result, not evidence for or against robustness. The full record is in `../audit/P4_ROBUSTNESS_COMPUTATIONAL_GATE_R3_006.md`; frozen P4 results are unchanged.
