# P5 Evidence-Gap Audit — V2 Recovery R3

## Current Evidence Is Sufficient For

P5 supports a complete, reproducible external comparison of four predeclared transparent EEG/EMG LDA conditions in a 24-subject, all-11-class, MI-only Jeong cohort. It supports the corrected within-source conclusion that no fusion rule outperformed EEG-only after Holm adjustment.

## Gaps That Remain

| Gap | Why P5 R2 cannot answer it | Required future evidence | Current status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Full 25-subject external cohort | One source file remains unavailable | Source-complete official release or a separately selected source; new protocol | YELLOW |
| Mechanism for EMG-only ordering | No residual-muscle, strategy, or signal-quality adjudication | Predeclared physiological/quality measurement study | RED |
| Motor-execution performance | P5 contains MI only | Separate ME protocol with preserved MI/ME distinction | YELLOW |
| General fusion advantage | One transparent LDA family only | New registered architecture/fusion study with fair baselines | YELLOW |
| Cross-source agreement with Cho2017 | Datasets/pipelines/tasks differ | Prospective harmonized multi-source study | YELLOW |
| Clinical/prosthetic relevance | Healthy volunteer MI data and offline endpoint | Appropriate device/clinical evidence | RED |

## Non-Remedies

The above gaps cannot be addressed by restoring `sub-23` from an unverified source, narrowing the label set, removing difficult subjects, retuning LDA, adding deep fusion, or borrowing target-subject calibration. All such actions would change the scientific question and require a new protocol/evidence namespace.
