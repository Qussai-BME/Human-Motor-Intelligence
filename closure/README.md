# Closure Records Index

Files in this directory record the closure audits of the programme. Numbers 02 and 09 are not used in this package. Documents dated before 2026-10-06 keep their dated wording; where they predate the executed P6 C1 result or the revised Paper 2, the current status is given in `P6_FINAL_STATUS.md` and `papers/P2_SUPERSESSION_NOTICE.md`.

Added in v1.2.0: `11_P6_C2_CLOSURE_REPORT.md` (audit of C1 and C2 follow-up). Dated documents that describe P6 C1 as complete with a non-discriminative endpoint keep their wording; their current interpretation is in `../papers/P6_SUPERSESSION_NOTICE.md`.
